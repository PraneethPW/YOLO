# Landing artwork

`frontend/public/road-hero.webp` is original artwork generated once using the built-in ImageGen tool. It serves only as the landing-page background.

Prompt:

> Cinematic top-down oblique view of a multilane highway interchange at night. Dark charcoal blue road surfaces and white road markings. Fine cool teal roadside light and a few warm orange headlights, sparse real cars. Realistic architectural aerial photography. Expansive wide landscape composition, roads sweeping diagonally through the frame. High contrast with subtle photographic grain. Detail should feel like a credible road network, with sweeping ramps, separated lanes and barriers. No crash, no incident scene, no labels, no text, no interface, no logos, no watermark. This is an atmospheric background, not incident footage or app data.

Real camera frames and incident evidence never use this asset.
